## Topics & Techniques Summary

**Sequences** — the guide covers all major limit evaluation techniques: algebraic simplification by dividing by the dominant power, L'Hôpital's Rule for indeterminate forms, the log trick for exponential-form limits, the Squeeze Theorem for bounded oscillating terms, and the Monotone Convergence Theorem for proving convergence without computing a limit.

**Series** — all standard convergence tests are covered in order of typical exam priority: Divergence Test (always first), Geometric and p-Series recognition, Integral Test with substitution, Direct and Limit Comparison, Ratio and Root Tests for factorial/power terms, Alternating Series Test, and the distinction between absolute and conditional convergence.
